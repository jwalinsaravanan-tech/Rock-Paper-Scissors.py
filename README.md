What they do: 
Rock Paper Scissors - play a short game of rock paper scissors with the computer.
Calculator - calculates two numbers (You can either add(+), subtract(-), divide(/) or multiply(x)).
Saying hello to person - Enter you name and the computer says hello back to you.
Coin Toss Simulator 1 - You have to choose between Heads or Tails, guess wrong and u loose...
Coin Toss Simulator 2 - Tell the computer how many times you want to toss the coin and it tells you the results (list of tails or heads in the amount you selected to toss the coin).
Coin Toss Simulator 2 (specified) - Tell the computer how many times you want to toss the coin and it tells you the results (Heads: )(Tails: ).
Number guessing game - Guess the number the computer has chosen but you only get 5 chances!
